# Website 03: Capsule — Travel Tickets, Bookings & Documents

A pixel-accurate frontend recreation of the modern travel documents & booking management landing page.

## Route
- `/travel-landing`

## Features & Interactions
1. **Glassmorphism Floating Pill Nav**:
   - Capsule logo with dual-orb geometry
   - Interactive dropdowns for Company & Help
   - Working Login and Pricing modal triggers
2. **Hero Typography with Depth Stacking**:
   - Giant bold headline with trailing line fade layered behind the floating cards
3. **4 Interactive Floating Cards**:
   - **Card 1 (Notes & Manager)**: Flight/ticket management overview with interactive *"Add your stuff"* modal
   - **Card 2 (Norway Trip)**: Upcoming 12-day vacation passport card
   - **Card 3 (Jet Black Flight Pass)**: Obsidian glass ticket for WE1786 USA trip with animated flight path
   - **Card 4 (Boarding Pass)**: New Jersey to California flight details with live radar status tracker
4. **Organic Motion**:
   - Continuous subtle floating physics on all cards
   - Hover lifts with rotation straightening and high elevation
   - Full touch & tap responsiveness for mobile (375px)
5. **Atmospheric Sky Backdrop**:
   - High-fidelity cloud layering framing the viewport
6. **Download for iOS CTA**:
   - Apple download button with interactive download progress and celebratory confetti
